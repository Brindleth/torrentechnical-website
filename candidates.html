FAQs

Common questions.

Are you a typical recruitment agency?

No. Most agencies are sales-led with engineers nowhere in the process. Torren Technical is run by an engineer who personally reviews every candidate and brief.

What disciplines do you cover?

Electronic test, automation/PLC, embedded, industrial electrical, mechanical fitting, instrumentation, HVAC, welding, construction trades, project management, property maintenance, plant ops, and adjacent roles. If your discipline isn't listed, register anyway — we'll tell you honestly.

Do I need to be in Australia?

For most roles, yes — with valid work rights. We don't provide immigration advice (that's MARA-regulated), but we can work with candidates already in-country or with confirmed sponsorship arrangements.

How long until I hear back?

Within 5 business days for the initial review. If we have a current role match, sooner. If not, we'll keep your details on file with your consent and reach out when something fits.

Will my current employer find out?

No. We never share your details with any employer without your explicit per-role consent. Your CV doesn't leave our system without you saying "yes, send it to that company".

Can I delete my data?

Yes, at any time. Email candidates@torrentechnical.com with the subject "Delete my data" and we'll action it within 5 business days.
