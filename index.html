002 · Process

How we work.

A five-stage engagement model. Each step is documented, each output is reviewable, each gate is signed off before the next begins.

01

Technical Brief Review

We read the engineering spec, scope of work or position description before writing the recruitment brief. Vague briefs are challenged early.

02

Engineer-Led Screening

Candidates are technically vetted against the actual criteria: software, tooling, clearance status, equipment exposure, domain depth.

03

Structured Shortlist

3–5 candidates delivered with technical write-ups: fit assessment, salary, availability, work rights, motivations and known risks.

04

Interview Coordination

Scheduling, technical test logistics, reference checks, offer negotiation support. We stay involved through to the start date.

05

Pipeline Building

For ongoing programs, we map and maintain a longer-term talent pipeline against your roadmap — not just the immediate role.
