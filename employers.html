Engagement

From brief to shortlist in five working days.

A documented, four-stage sourcing engagement. No black-box recruiting, no surprise invoices.

1

Technical brief

30-minute call to walk through the role, the team, the actual technical requirements, and the dealbreakers. We push back where briefs are unrealistic — and tell you what would make them fillable.

2

Engineer-led sourcing

Targeted sourcing across our network, LinkedIn, industry forums and existing pool. Every candidate is qualified by engineers against the spec — not auto-matched on keywords.

3

Structured shortlist

Typically 3–5 candidates with technical write-ups: fit assessment, salary, availability, work rights, motivations and known risks. No filler, no padding.

4

End-to-end coordination

Interview scheduling, technical test logistics, reference checks, offer negotiation. We stay involved until the candidate starts — and follow up after.

Pricing

Three commercial models. Your choice.

Published, not negotiated in the dark. No exclusivity locks. No hidden fees. You pay for delivered value.

💼

Retainer

From $3,500 + GST per role

For critical hires, multiple roles, or ongoing pipeline. Includes prioritised sourcing, weekly progress reports, market mapping where required.

📋

Per-shortlist

$800–$1,500 + GST per shortlist

Single-role engagement. Fixed-price shortlist of pre-screened candidates. No placement fee. Best for buyers who want to evaluate the work before committing further.

🎯

Contingent

12–18% of first-year salary

Pay only on outcome. Standard agency commercial, but with engineer-led screening. Replacement guarantee included.

All pricing excludes GST. Detailed terms in proposal. Zero charge to candidates under any model.
