How it works

From brief to shortlist in 5 business days.

1

Brief

30-min call to walk through the role, the team, the actual technical requirements, and the dealbreakers. We push back where briefs are unrealistic.

2

Source

Targeted sourcing across our network, LinkedIn, industry forums, and existing candidate pool. Every candidate is technically pre-screened before reaching you.

3

Shortlist

Typically 3–5 candidates with detailed write-ups: technical fit, salary, availability, work rights, motivations, concerns. No filler.

4

Coordinate

Interview scheduling, technical test coordination (where applicable), reference checks, offer negotiation support. We stay involved until the candidate starts.

Pricing

Three commercial models. Your choice.

No exclusivity locks. No hidden fees. Pricing is structured so you only pay for value delivered.

💼

Retainer

From $3,500 + GST per role

Best for: critical hires, multiple roles, ongoing pipeline. Includes prioritised sourcing, weekly progress reports, market mapping if needed.

📋

Per-shortlist

$800–$1,500 + GST per shortlist

Best for: single role, want to evaluate before committing further. Fixed-price shortlist of pre-screened candidates. No placement fee.

🎯

Contingent

12–18% of first-year salary

Best for: roles where you only want to pay on outcome. Standard agency model, but with engineer-led screening. Replacement guarantee included.

All pricing excludes GST. Detailed terms provided in proposal. No charge to candidates under any model.
