About

Engineers running a recruitment firm — not the other way around.

Most technical recruiters can't read a circuit diagram, a piping schematic or a structural drawing. Most engineers can't sell. Torren Technical sits in the gap.

The firm

Engineer-led from the start.

Torren Technical was founded by technically trained engineers with industry experience across electronics, defence, controls, and industrial environments. The firm exists for one reason: to fix the gap between technical employers who need precise sourcing and recruiters who treat every CV the same.

Our consultants come from engineering, trades and technical backgrounds. We hire technical people to recruit technical people — and we train every consultant to read the spec before writing the brief.

Torren Technical is built to scale: every workflow, every shortlist, every compliance step is documented and repeatable. The quality of the recruitment doesn't depend on a single person.

  • Focus: Australian technical recruitment
  • Sectors: Engineering, Industrial Trades, Construction, Mining & Resources
  • Based: Australia-wide
  • Approach: Engineer-first, technically credible
  • Compliance: Privacy Act 1988, Spam Act 2003, APP-aligned
How we work

What "engineer-led" actually means.

📐

Spec before brief

Our consultants read the technical specification, scope of work, or job description before writing the recruitment brief. Vague briefs get clarified — they don't get blasted to LinkedIn.

🔍

Technical screening

Candidates are screened against actual technical criteria: tickets held, equipment used, software experience, clearance status. Not just keyword matching.

⚖

Transparent commercial models

Retainer, per-shortlist, or contingent — clients pick what fits the role and budget. Pricing is published, not negotiated in the dark.

🔒

Compliance baked in

Privacy Act 1988 consent collected at intake. Spam Act 2003 compliant outreach. APP-aligned data handling. Candidates always own their information.

🇦🇺

Australian-first

AU-based candidates with verified work rights as standard. International candidates are clearly flagged where relevant. No surprises at the offer stage.

📊

Honest reporting

Clients see what we see. If a role isn't attracting suitable candidates, we explain why and adjust the brief — not pad the shortlist with filler.

What we don't do

The things you won't find here.

Plenty of recruitment agencies will promise the world. Here's what Torren Technical explicitly doesn't do — because doing them badly is worse than not doing them at all.

  • We don't charge candidates. Ever. For anything.
  • We don't provide immigration advice. That's MARA-regulated.
  • We don't do CV blasting. Generic shortlists waste everyone's time.
  • We don't oversell. If a role is hard to fill, we'll say why — and what to change.
  • We don't lock clients in. No exclusivity unless mutually agreed in writing.
