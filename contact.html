Privacy & data

Your information, your control.

Torren Technical operates in line with the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. We collect only what's necessary to provide our service, and only with your consent.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your data, email info@torrentechnical.com with the relevant request and our team will action it within 5 business days.

  • Consent first. Nothing collected without explicit opt-in.
  • No CV blasting. Candidate details never go to an employer without per-role consent.
  • Data deletion on request. 5 business day turnaround.
  • Spam Act 2003 compliant. Every email includes a working unsubscribe.
  • No candidate fees. Free to register, free to be matched, free to be placed.
